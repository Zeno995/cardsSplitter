🃏 CARDS SPLITTER - The perfect app for your game nights! 🎴

Tired of keeping track of scores by hand during card games? Cards Splitter is the ideal solution to manage debts and credits between players in a simple, fast, and fun way!

✨ KEY FEATURES

🎮 Shared Game Sessions
Create a session and invite your friends with a simple code. All participants see movements in real-time!

💰 Easy Movement Tracking
Record payments and winnings with just a few taps. The app automatically calculates who owes what to whom.

🎲 Built-in Game Modes
Support for classic Italian holiday card games:
• Sette e mezzo (Seven and a Half)
• Bestia
• Trentuno / Cucù
• And many more!

📊 Automatic Balance Calculation
Instantly view each player's balance. No more arguments about who needs to pay!

🤝 Debt Simplification
The smart algorithm minimizes the number of transactions needed to settle all accounts.
